# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Finance and operations staff who receive funding spreadsheets, prepare product allocations and invoice documents, and review the final workbook at a desk. A business owner reviews and decides whether each candidate result should be adopted.

## Product Purpose

Help people process one funding batch at a time. Users upload the balance and reference files for a batch, inspect the source rows, enter product parameters, run an exact allocation, preview the candidate workbooks, and decide whether to adopt or discard the result. Keep the input version, candidate output, and next batch traceable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, approachable. The interface should make amounts and decisions easy to find, with direct Chinese labels and visible consequences for each action.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense control-room dashboards, decorative metric card grids, generic chat-first layouts, hidden spreadsheet details, animated finance graphics, and technical agent or storage terminology in routine user flows.

## Design Principles

- Put the current batch, its funding files, its exact allocation, and its candidate result in one workspace.
- Make the source-to-candidate-to-adopted version path visible and keep the original upload accessible.
- Let users inspect spreadsheet rows and allocation amounts before they make a decision.
- Keep AI suggestions reviewable and separate from deterministic money calculations and user decisions.
- Preserve rules and traceability while shortening routine navigation and copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text contrast, labeled controls, keyboard-visible focus, text and symbols alongside status colors, responsive tables, reduced-motion support, and explicit loading, empty, and error states.
